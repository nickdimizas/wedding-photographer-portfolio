About Me

Born and raised in Athens, Greece, from a young age I felt safe in the art of observation. Street photography became naturally a part of my everyday life, teaching me to adapt in every situation.

I believe in the purity of spontaneous moments and expressions, intervening only when necessary. Every place becomes a unique studio — from islands and mountains to cities and villages — always challenging me to capture the most vivid and emotional frames.
